Crafting Purity from Seed to Serum

Hizqeel Botanics emerged from an apothecary studio dedicated to bridging ancestral herbal practices with rigorous organic chemistry. Our mission is to restore the connection between daily wellness rituals and the vitality of living plants.

We do not believe in shelf-stabilizing short cuts. Every batch is formulated fresh through whole-plant extractions to retain fragile trace compounds, antioxidants, and terpenes.

The Core Pillars

Ethical Sourcing

100% certified organic botanicals sourced directly from cooperative farms committed to rich topsoil vitality.

Whole-Herb Extraction

Slow mechanical cold-press and ultrasonic infusion without high temperatures that degrade plant proteins.

Pure Compounding

Unadulterated formulas free of artificial colors, stabilizing gums, and chemical preservatives.

Our Compounding Journey

  • 1. Hand Selection & Curing: Raw herbs are collected during peak dawn hours to capture concentrated essential oils, then shade-dried.
  • 2. Low-Temperature Extraction: Extractions rest for weeks in unreactive glass vessels to pull deep phytonutrients gently and naturally.
  • 3. Rigorous Small-Batch Testing: Every individual yield is checked for aroma, viscosity, and chemical purity prior to bottle-sealing.
  • 4. Amber Storage: Bottles are filled, labeled, and sealed in light-proof UV apothecary glass for optimum shelf life.
